# 出海试衣间 / Design

## Style Prompt
温暖、克制的品牌工作室。奶油纸色画布，松绿色文字，细线与大幅留白。左右对照像打版工作台，商品卡片像一张实体吊牌。事实来源以可点击的小标签呈现。
## Colors
- #f4f2e9 canvas
- #173e35 ink and brand
- #ffffff surface
- #dce8cd soft accent
- #9b492b warning
## Typography
Georgia / SimSun for display; Microsoft YaHei / system sans for UI. Monospace for step numbers.
## Motion
Short 250ms fades and translations. Respect reduced motion. Video uses 600ms crossfades and 350–600ms entrances.
## What NOT to Do
No blue-purple gradients, glass effects, fabricated scores, invented customer research, unlabelled model output, or tiny body copy.
